================================================================================
ENTERPRISE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) PLAYBOOK
================================================================================

1. MARKETING BUDGET & AD SPEND DEVIATION
- Condition: Sudden volume drop correlated with marketing budget cut or ad pause.
- Root Cause: Paid acquisition channel deprioritization or campaign throttling.
- Action: Immediately notify Growth Marketing team to unpause regional campaigns and restore target CPM/CPC ad spend baselines within 2 hours.
- Owner: Head of Digital Marketing

2. PRICING DRIFT / UNAUTHORIZED DISCOUNTING
- Condition: Negative price effect resulting in revenue loss exceeding target margin.
- Root Cause: Regional price cut, expired promotional code override, or competitor matching.
- Action: Flag pricing mismatch in ERP, freeze manual discount overrides, and trigger automated margin compliance review.
- Owner: Commercial Strategy & Pricing Ops

3. PAYMENT GATEWAY TIMEOUT / 502 API FAILURE
- Condition: Sharp transaction volume drop with normal web traffic.
- Root Cause: Primary payment gateway connection drop or API timeout.
- Action: Rollback checkout module to previous stable version and route regional traffic to secondary backup processor.
- Owner: IT Engineering / Site Reliability

4. COLD START / LAUNCH PRODUCT VOLATILITY
- Condition: Sparse history KPI (<14 days) showing >10% variance against category baseline.
- Root Cause: Early launch elasticity, onboarding funnel friction, or initial stock stocking delays.
- Action: Deploy launch monitoring protocol; request 48-hour manual analytics tracking before modifying standard price/volume targets.
- Owner: Product Operations

5. UNEXPLAINED OPERATIONAL ANOMALY
- Condition: Metric crash with zero matching infrastructure or marketing error telemetry.
- Root Cause: Unknown external/system factor.
- Action: Programmatic abstention required. Do not execute automated remediation. Escalate incident ticket to Tier-3 Data Analyst.
- Owner: BI Incident Response Team
